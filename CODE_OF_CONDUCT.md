# Code of Conduct

OpenRoto is intended to be a practical, collaborative open-source project.

Participants are expected to:

- discuss technical disagreements respectfully;
- critique code and ideas rather than people;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats and personal attacks;
- respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information;
- keep issues and pull requests focused on the project;
- follow applicable licenses when sharing code, models, footage or other assets.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content and may restrict participation when behavior repeatedly disrupts the project or puts contributors at risk.

If a conduct issue cannot reasonably be handled in public, contact the repository owner privately.
